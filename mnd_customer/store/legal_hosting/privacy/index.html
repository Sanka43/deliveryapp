Privacy Policy

MND Delivery · Last updated: 6 August 2026 · support@mnddelivery.com

Who we are

MND Delivery provides food, grocery, ride, and local jobs services through the MND customer app.

Data we collect

Account data (phone via OTP, name, email if provided, profile photo), delivery addresses, order and trip history, location when permitted, push tokens, and app usage needed to operate the service.

How we use data

To authenticate you, fulfil orders and rides, show maps and ETAs, send notifications you opt into, prevent fraud, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share data with vendors and riders as needed to fulfil your order or trip, and with Firebase/Google infrastructure. Legal disclosure may occur when required by law.

reCAPTCHA (web)

On the web app, phone sign-in is protected by Google reCAPTCHA. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. reCAPTCHA may collect hardware and software information for abuse prevention; that data is used under Google’s policies.

Retention & deletion

Delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. That removes your Auth account and customer profile data from backends we control; some anonymised operational records may remain.
