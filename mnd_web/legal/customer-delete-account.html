Delete Your MND Account

MND Customer app · Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Delete your account from within the app

Open the MND app, sign in, and go to Settings > Delete account, then confirm the deletion. This is immediate and does not require contacting support.

2. What gets deleted

When you confirm account deletion, we immediately remove:

  • Your profile photo
  • Your saved addresses
  • Your profile details (name, email, phone number)
  • Your sign-in credentials (your account can no longer be signed into)

3. What we retain

We keep records of past orders (order contents, amounts, and delivery details) for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes, as required by applicable law. This data is no longer linked to an account you can sign in to.

4. Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to your phone number or the app itself, email masterndelivery111@gmail.com from the address or phone number on the account and request account deletion. We will verify ownership and process the request, normally within 7 business days.

5. Contact

For questions about account deletion or your data, contact masterndelivery111@gmail.com.
